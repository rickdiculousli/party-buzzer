# The board's scales: the closest line and timeline labels

Both scales on the board, the buzz timeline and the closest line, draw a connector from each point to its label, and every label must stay readable and attributable.

- `lineScale` spans the answer and at least the middle 90 % of guesses. A wild guess would squash the rest into a dot, so the outermost guesses at an end are parked when the gap in from them exceeds twice (`WILD`) the span of the rest, taking the deepest such gap within the cap so two jokers at one end go together. At most a twentieth of the guesses at each end are parked, and at least one once there are three; two guesses are never trimmed. An 8 % margin keeps end marks off the edges, and an all-equal line gets padding. A parked guess's pointer is an inline SVG arrow, because the display face has no arrow glyphs.
- Guess labels sit in lanes above the line so close guesses stack rather than overprint. Every label covers the stems beneath it (`z-index: 1`), so a lane is chosen that also keeps clear of the dot of any higher label and of any lower label over its own dot; otherwise a stem vanishes behind a box and the label reads as a neighbour's. Overprinting is chosen only when no lane is free of it, and then the least crowded lane. Lanes are capped at `MAX_LANES` so the line never pushes the band off a short wall.
- The paid-points chip beside a winning label is out of flow until it lights, so through the hold a paid label is centred like the rest and gives nothing away. The won and second labels change colour with their own `closest-lit` and `closest-dim` keyframes delayed to `--light-at` with backwards fill, because the interest kit has no delayed, held change of colour.
- Timeline marks set `z-index: calc(10 - var(--lane))`, so a shallow label near the rail paints over the long pins of deeper marks reaching past it. The timeline is padded so marks at 0% and 100%, centred on the rail ends, have room to hang without clipping.
