# The finale is one schedule shared by every surface, staged on the board by translates

- `finaleTiers` gives the distinct places 1 to 3 counted the way phones count their own place (one plus the rows strictly ahead), so ties share a tier and a tie for 2nd leaves no 3rd.
- `finaleStep` turns time since `setlist.spentAt` into the ceremony's step on each surface's synced clock (build, then 3rd, then 2nd, then the winner for good), skipping places with no tier, so the board and phones call each place together and a reload or late surface lands on the settled winner instead of replaying it. 4th place and below are called with the winner (`finaleCalled`), and a phone says nothing before its place is called. A surface with no stamp treats the finale as settled.
- `FINALE_SCHEDULE.build` must equal the CSS `--finale-build-dur` on `.board__finale-flash`; `client/tunables.test.ts` holds them equal. A board mounting mid-build starts the flash with a negative `animationDelay` of the elapsed time, so flash and step change end together.
- On the wide board the wall spans both columns and the sidebar sits translated off its right edge through the build, the podium steps and the winner's first seconds, because the standings (1st on top) would spoil the ceremony. Then the sidebar slides in and the bands move left by half its width; both are `translate` transitions, so the GPU does them without relayout and the standings leave the way they arrive when Replay starts again. A board mounting on a settled finale starts in place (a first style never transitions), and an undo of Replay (`.is-settled`) skips the wait. On Replay the bands snap back instead of sliding, because a band mid-translate would become the flash's containing block while the lane is empty.
- The build is one element and one animation painting its own background; the spotlight's colour and radius are registered `@property` values so the gradient animates without an extra layer.
- A podium step's place medal and score, and the winner's recap band, are out of flow, so the name keeps the lane's centre and the winner lands where the steps stood. Podium delays are set two classes deep so they outrank the interest kit's `animation` shorthand.
- The confetti cannons are portalled onto `.board__wall`, not the stage, because the stage shifts for the sidebar and would carry the cannons' edges with it. `.board__cannons` is a zero-height line across the wall's middle so each cannon fires from the midpoint of its edge.
