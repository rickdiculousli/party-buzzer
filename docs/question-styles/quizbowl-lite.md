# Quizbowl-lite

Pyramid questions with every clue pointing at one answer, trimmed down for a
casual party. Reference pack: [packs/quizbowl-lite.txt](../../packs/quizbowl-lite.txt).
Accepted-answer rules are shared across styles; see the [index](README.md).

## Answers

- Every answer is something nearly everyone at a party knows: a famous object,
  person, work, place or idea. The difficulty lives in the clues, never in the
  answer.

## Structure

- Three fragments per question, hardest clue first:
  1. An obscure but true fact that only a well-read player would know.
  2. A mid-level clue that a fair share of the room would get.
  3. A giveaway that starts with "Name this …" and that almost everyone gets.
- Early fragments reward early buzzes. A player who waits for the giveaway
  still has a fair chance.

## Opening word

- The first fragment starts with "This thing", "This man" or "This group"
  ("These things" for plurals). Casual players don't know the quizbowl
  convention that "this" marks the answer. Opening on the same cue word every
  time teaches them.
- Use "thing" for everything that isn't a person or group, including works,
  processes and forces. A specific word ("This president", "This painting")
  gives too much away in the hardest clue.
- Later fragments refer back with it / he / its / they. The giveaway can name
  the exact category ("Name this Shakespeare tragedy …").

## Language

- Keep the quizbowl pronoun style and the "Name this …" giveaway.
- Leave out "For 10 points", prompt instructions, pronunciation guides and
  stacked relative clauses. Sentences should be easy to follow when read
  aloud once.
- Avoid category words in a clue that contradict the opening. For example,
  don't say "sister ship" after "This thing".

## Difficulty ramp

- Values rise in pairs, 100 to 500. A higher value means harder first two
  clues, not a less famous answer.
